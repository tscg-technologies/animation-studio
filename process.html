Step-by-Step Breakdown

THE 5 PRODUCTION STAGES

Each stage is structured with clear creative deliverables so your story, visual tone and animation style stay on track from start to finish.

Stage 01
Foundation & Strategy

Stage 1: Concept & Discovery

Every project begins by exploring your core idea, communication goals and viewer context—establishing the right visual tone and animation discipline before production begins.

  • Understand the project idea
  • Identify the target audience
  • Define the visual direction
  • Establish the animation style
Stage 02
Narrative Blueprint

Stage 2: Script & Storyboard

We translate the core concept into a structured narrative script and sequential visual storyboard panels—mapping out camera angles, scene transitions and pacing beats.

  • Story development
  • Script preparation
  • Scene planning
  • Storyboard creation
Stage 03
World & Asset Craft

Stage 3: Design & Visual Development

Once the storyboard structure is aligned, custom artwork is created—including character design turnarounds, illustrated backgrounds, colour scripts and production-ready visual assets.

  • Character design
  • Background design
  • Colour and style development
  • Visual asset preparation
Stage 04
Motion Execution

Stage 4: Animation & Production

Static illustrations, 3D scenes and graphic layouts are brought to life with expressive character movement, dynamic camera sweeps, kinetic typography and synchronized sound timing.

  • Character movement
  • Scene animation
  • Motion graphics
  • Sound and visual synchronization
Stage 05
Polish & Master Export

Stage 5: Final Review & Delivery

We conduct final visual and audio quality checks, apply agreed refinements, complete high-resolution rendering and export master files in your selected aspect ratios.

  • Review
  • Refinements
  • Final rendering
  • Export in selected formats
Workflow Clarity

PRODUCTION PROCESS QUESTIONS

Understanding how creative collaboration and stage approvals work during an animation project.

Storyboards establish the visual sequence and pacing, while styleframes show the exact final look, colour palette and character design. Aligning on these visual blueprints early ensures smooth production once Stage 4 (Animation & Production) begins.
Every animation project varies in length, visual complexity, character count and aspect ratio requirements. Rather than a one-size-fits-all schedule, milestones and review checkpoints are scoped collaboratively during Stage 1 (Concept & Discovery).
Final deliverables can be exported for Horizontal widescreen (16:9), Vertical mobile reels (9:16), Square social feeds (1:1) or custom display dimensions depending on the formats planned at the start of the project.
